About

The Studio

A & R Spaces is an architectural renovation studio founded by Ruchi Shah and Ashay Shah, dedicated to transforming residential spaces through meticulous design and exceptional craftsmanship.

Our approach is rooted in architectural precision, where every detail is considered and every material is selected for its timeless quality. We believe that great design endures—creating spaces that feel both contemporary and eternal.

Serving clients throughout Illinois, we specialize in high-end residential renovations that honor the architectural integrity of each home while introducing modern functionality and refined aesthetics.
